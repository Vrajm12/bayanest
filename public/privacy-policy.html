6.

Consent

Under Section 6 of the DPDP Act, 2023, consent given by a Data Principal must be free, specific, informed, unconditional, and unambiguous, expressed through a clear affirmative action.

  • Affirmative Action: When you voluntarily submit your contact details via our enquiry form or initiate contact by email/phone, you perform an affirmative action requesting communication from Bayanest Realty.
  • Purpose Limitation: Your consent is limited strictly to the purpose of responding to your inquiry and providing relevant consultancy information.
  • Right of Revocation: You hold the statutory right to withdraw your consent at any point in time.
  • Consequences of Withdrawal: If you withdraw consent, Bayanest Realty will cease processing your personal data for that purpose. Consequently, we may no longer be able to provide follow-up information or complete your requested advisory consultation.

8.

Data Principal Rights

Under Chapter III of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you (as the "Data Principal") possess enforceable statutory rights regarding your personal data:

Right to Access
The right to obtain a summary of personal data being processed, the processing activities undertaken, and the identities of any Data Fiduciaries/Processors with whom data has been shared.
Right to Correction & Completion
The right to request the correction of inaccurate or misleading personal data, completion of incomplete data, and updating of out-of-date information.
Right to Erasure
The right to request deletion of your personal data when it is no longer necessary for the specified purpose or upon withdrawal of consent, subject to statutory retention mandates.
Right to Grievance Redressal
The right to readily accessible grievance redressal mechanisms regarding any act or omission of Bayanest Realty in respect of its performance under data protection law.
Right to Nominate
The right to nominate, in accordance with applicable rules, another individual who shall, in the event of death or incapacity of the Data Principal, exercise the rights of the Data Principal.

Data Retention

In accordance with the principle of storage limitation under the DPDP Act, 2023, personal data is retained only for as long as necessary to satisfy the purpose for which it was collected, or as required by applicable laws and regulations:

  • Enquiry & Consultation Records: Retained for the duration of the active consultation and a reasonable follow-up period thereafter to ensure customer satisfaction and service continuity.
  • Statutory Mandates: Where transaction records or regulatory compliance documentation must be preserved under MahaRERA, the Companies Act, or taxation laws, retention periods adhere to the requisite statutory timeframes.
  • Erasure Upon Request: Personal data is deleted or securely anonymized once the purpose is achieved or upon a valid erasure request, provided no conflicting legal retention obligation applies.
14.

Data Security

Bayanest Realty implements reasonable technical, organizational, and physical safeguards appropriate to the nature of the data processed, to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction of personal data:

  • Encryption in Transit: All web traffic to and from this website is encrypted using industry-standard Hypertext Transfer Protocol Secure (HTTPS / TLS).
  • Access Control: Personal data received through enquiries is accessible only to authorized personnel bound by confidentiality duties.
  • Static Architecture: By employing a static website without dynamic database endpoints or user authentication systems, we minimize exposure to common web application attack vectors.

While we implement appropriate safeguards, no method of transmission over the internet is completely infallible. Visitors are encouraged to exercise prudence when sharing confidential or sensitive particulars over public networks.
